Fields§

§name: String

The name of the route

Also known as Rosenmei, 路線名.

§stations: Vec<Station>

What stations are included in the route

Also known as Eki, 駅.

§classes: Vec<Class>

The available train classes. E.g., local, express.

Also known as Ressyasyubetsu, 列車種別.

§diagrams: Vec<Diagram>

The diagrams included in this route. Each diagram is a timetable set.

Also known as Dia, ダイヤ.

§display_start_time: Time

When to start displaying times on the diagram page.

Also known as KitenJikoku, 起点時刻.

§comment: String

Also known as Comment.

§down_dia_alias: Option<String>

Also known as KudariDiaAlias, 下りダイヤ別名.

§up_dia_alias: Option<String>

Also known as NoboriDiaAlias, 上りダイヤ別名.

§diagram_station_interval_default: i32

Default vertical spacing (in the diagram’s Y-coordinate units) between adjacent stations on the diagram.

Also known as DiagramDgrYZahyouKyoriDefault.

§enable_operation: i32

Also known as EnableOperation.

§operation_number_reverse: bool

Whether operation numbers are shown in reverse order.

Also known as OperationNumberReverse.

§operation_crosses_start_time: bool

Whether operations are allowed to cross the diagram’s start time.

Also known as OperationCrossKitenJikoku.

§reference_diagram_index: Option<i32>

Index of the reference (baseline) diagram.

Also known as KijunDiaIndex.

§disable_hidden_class: bool

Whether to ignore classes marked as hidden.

Also known as DisableHiddenSyubetsu.
