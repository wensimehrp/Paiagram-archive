Route Timetable

A route timetable shows the times of trips running on a specific route. The first column is all stations in the route, and each column after the first column lists a trip travelling on the route. Each row displays when a trip arrives or depart from a station, or the platform/track the trip will be staying at. The route timetable tile in Paiagram is based on Japanse train route timetables (e.g. the table in https://but.tw/font/).

The table includes these elements:

⇂Non-stop. This trip passes this station but does not stop at this station
||Does not pass station. This trip travels somewhere else and does not pass nor stop at this station
..Not in operation. Trip hasn’t started its operation or has already ended its operation by this station and timepoint
1234Time, 12:34. Trip arrives at or departs from this station at this time.
936Time, 9:36. Trip arrives at or departs from this station at this time.
=====Ending mark. Trip ends its operation before or at this station
